Dev Log · Uncharted Rally

Uncharted Rally #007

15 April 2026 / 1 min read / Oliver Creese

AI overtaking and obstacle avoidance logic, a weather system and changing the tone mapper. Oh boy.

Advanced AI

Previously the AI drivers had no knowledge of other vehicles on the track — they would just try to follow the track spline the best they could. Now there is a lane system on the track that allows the AI to switch between lanes to avoid obstacles and also to try to overtake the player. It works by simply adding specific values to the left or right of the given spline position at distance x, and then using a collision box around the AI to detect when cars are near it so it can overtake when it is safe to do so.

Weather System

I have built a weather system using dynamic material instances and timelines that change the weather in real time. I also made a rain particle system that works with the terrain material to create puddles on the floor. With Unreal's new vegetation system I can also dynamically adjust wind speed on the foliage to create realistic stormy conditions.

Tone Mapper

Unreal's default tone mapper, ACES Filmic, is known for its high fidelity for film production, however it is synonymous in games for creating the 'Unreal Engine look'. To combat this I have swapped it for AGX tone mapping, which gives a noticeably bolder look and gives the game a more unique feel.
