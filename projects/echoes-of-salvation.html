Echoes Of Salvation

A fast-paced first person arena shooter where you play as a robot sent to destroy the alien threat that has taken over the surface of the planet. High-octane combat against hordes of insect and reptilian enemies, with an expansive arsenal of weapons, weapon mods and enhanced movement. Built in my second year of university.

Role Lead Programmer & Combat Designer
Engine Unreal Engine
Platform Windows
Controls Keyboard & Mouse

Check it out for yourself!

View on GitLab →

Key Features

Built around player feel: realistic weapon handling and total freedom of movement.

  • True first person locomotion
  • Procedural animations for recoil and weapon sway
  • Scalable weapon system using skeletal mesh components and a proper class inheritance hierarchy
  • Dash and double jump movement mechanics
  • Weapon mod system
  • Dynamic world space UI
  • Boss fight AI

Design

Every system was designed with player feel in mind — realistic weapon feel and freedom of movement. Each weapon serves a specific purpose, and the UI adopts a robotic interface style to boost immersion.

Inspired by Ultrakill and Destiny.

Development Challenges

True first person locomotion required mapping mouse input to spine bone rotations, so the camera and body always stay in sync.

Managing which mods fit which weapons led to a classification system within the weapon class hierarchy, which also drives each weapon's firing logic.

Gameplay Loop

  1. Select your weapons
  2. Choose your weapon mods
  3. Defeat waves of enemies
  4. Take down the final boss
